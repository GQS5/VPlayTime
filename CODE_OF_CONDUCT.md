# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in VPlayTime a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, trolling, insults, sexualized language, or unwelcome attention — in issues, PRs, discussions, or any project space.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels; many contributors run their first server here.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for serious or repeated behavior. Reports are handled privately and confidentially.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html) (v2.1).
